# Surface: Sonic Dominion desktop app (and phone remote)

Mode: Operate. Audience: streamers and voice-chat gamers firing clips mid-game, setting up banks between sessions. Task: fire sounds instantly; organise banks; trim and assign keys. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (lightest memory; standard web controls; no glow, no decorative motion).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every sound is a white-label test pressing you stamped yourself, filed in a crate, spun the second you hit the key. Refuses the grey rounded-card soundboard dashboard and the neon gamer panel.

OWN-WORLD: Matte sleeve-black shell (warm-neutral near black), cool white label stock for pad labels, kraft crate-divider brown for banks, one rubber-stamp red for live/active and void marks, and four saturated cue-sticker dots (yellow, green, blue, pink) as each pad's colour. Big Shoulders Display stamped caps for names and headings, Schibsted Grotesk for UI copy, Martian Mono for catalog numbers, times and keys. States are printed marks: hairline ring on hover, filled stamp when pressed, struck through when disabled, bracketed on focus.

STORY: The visitor sees their crate (banks) on the left, the active bank's pressings in the middle, and fires them with keys; the spinning label confirms what is on air.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sleeve-black top bar with the stamped wordmark, Monitor and Cable outputs and a red-stamped Stop all. Left rail: search, views, then kraft divider tabs with stamped bank letters, the active one pulled forward in white. Centre: stamped bank heading, then a grid of pads, each a black sleeve with a round white label (catalog number SD-01 = slot), cue sticker, stamped name, mono length/mode and a standard keycap. Right: editor. Bottom: now-playing chips.

FORM: White Label, position 3 of 7 grounded candidates (game-show board, broadcast graphics, white label, mixtape J-card, arcade marquee, trading cards, transit wayfinding); seed:5e9195551dcd9aca. Signature move: the playing pad's label spins at 33 1/3 rpm with a red stylus arc tracking progress; groove band width is proportional to clip length (raise from Labanotation); printed-mark states (raise from Centre-Rail); failed or missing pads keep a red VOID stamp until opened (raise from Boarding Pass).
